A top-level `path` (GeoJSON LineString) WORKS and is priced along the path with live traffic: sending the baseline route's own 299 points returned 39 min / 30252 m, max deviation 0 m (baseline 39 min / 30252 m). A path priced route has no usual time of its own (the "historical" request returns the live time), so the app carries the baseline of the route she is on. TomTom also limits requests per second (429 when 5+ calls burst).
- **Fixes (`src/lib/route-commitment.ts`, wired in `src/routes/index.tsx`; `via` = path in `src/lib/drive.functions.ts`; ramp points in `buildManeuvers`; tests `src/lib/route-commitment.test.ts`):**
  - Stickiness: a refresh that leaves the road on screen (>= 500 m apart) is priced against staying: the same request with the whole remaining current road as the `path`, same live traffic and start. Switch only if it saves >= max(5 min, 15% of the time left on her road). Below that she stays, and the priced stay route (live ETA, traffic, incidents, maneuvers) replaces the stale one. If staying can't be priced or doesn't follow her road (a closure ahead, TomTom error), the fresh route is accepted: the app fails toward rerouting, never toward a stale route.
  - Proposal: HUD "Faster route / Via Kamehameha Hwy — saves 7 min. Accident on H-1 near ʻAiea." plus one natural spoken line ("Faster via Kamehameha Highway, saves 7 minutes. ..."). The reason comes only from TomTom data on the stretch the other route avoids (incident with its stretch > delayed named stretch > no reason); shared road is ignored. An incident notice for her road always wins the HUD slot.
  - Respect her choice: pass the exit and stay on the road and Nalu switches to a route that continues on her road, says/shows "Staying on H-1 — about +N min" once (HUD label "Route kept"), and does not propose that detour again for 10 min unless it saves 5+ min more than when she declined. Her road ahead is taken from a route she was already given (never guessed from her heading); with no such route the fresh route is followed and nothing is claimed. Known gap: if the very first route of a drive already takes the exit there is no earlier geometry of her road, so after a decline the app follows TomTom's fresh route.
  - Declined-exit detection: exits/ramps/forks carry the route's own full-resolution ramp points (700 m). Past the exit point (and past it on the route itself), 3 consecutive good fixes (accuracy <= 25 m) count as declined when they are > 40 m from the ramp line AND > 35 degrees off its heading at >= 3 m/s, or > 60 m from it when slower. Tests use curving and looping ramps (slow ramp-taking with 15 m GPS drift is not flagged).
  - Cost control: at most one "price staying" request per 100 s and none for 5 min after a failed one for the same detour; no historical-baseline call for path-priced routes; the server pauses path requests for 10 min after a TomTom 400; one retry after 800 ms on a 429.
  - Transit re-planning: while a drive is navigated the planner minute moves at most every 5 min (`throttledPlanMinute`); on the drive's end it follows the live minute immediately, so departed trips drop; the trip list isn't shown during navigation. Drive time/ETA/verdict queries are untouched.
  - Diagnostics (no coordinates): `route_proposed {fromVia,toVia,savesMin,reason,text}`, `route_kept {savesMin,why}`, `route_declined {maneuver,road,text}`, `route_switched {via,plusMin,text}`; `voice` events carry `text`.
